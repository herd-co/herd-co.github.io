Careers · Bartlett, Nebraska

Work that starts before sunrise and matters all day.

Feedyard Operations Intern (Summer 2027)

Internship May–August 2027 Paid hourly, based on experience Housing assistance

A rotation through every part of the yard: pen riding and processing, the feed mill and bunk reading, animal health with our consulting vet, and a close-out project in the office. Best fit for animal science, ag business, or agronomy students entering their junior or senior year.

What you will do
  • Ride pens and learn to spot sick cattle early
  • Process incoming cattle: tagging, vaccination, and records
  • Read bunks and follow the ration from mill to pen
  • Complete a lot close-out analysis and present it to the team

Pen Rider

Full time Start immediately Pay based on experience Benefits

Check assigned pens daily on horseback, pull and treat cattle with the health crew, and keep accurate records. Horses provided. Experience with cattle and low-stress handling required; feedyard experience preferred.

Requirements
  • Confident horseback rider in all weather
  • Able to identify sick or injured cattle and follow treatment protocols
  • Valid driver's license
  • Willing to work a rotating weekend schedule
Apply

Feed Truck Driver / Mill Operator

Full time Start immediately Pay based on experience Benefits

Deliver rations to bunks on schedule, operate loaders and mixers at the mill, and help receive commodities. No CDL required for on-yard driving. Mechanical aptitude and a clean safety record matter more than years of experience.

Accounting & Finance Intern (Summer 2027)

Internship May–August 2027 Paid hourly, based on experience Office based

Work directly with the controller on month-end close, feed billing, customer statements, and cattle inventory reconciliation in our feedyard accounting system. Accounting, finance, or ag economics majors preferred. You will leave knowing how a cattle lot turns into a financial statement.

What you will do
  • Prepare month-end liability schedules and account reconciliations
  • Reconcile packer settlements against yard records
  • Build reporting in Excel from the feedyard system
  • Sit in on lender, insurance, and marketing conversations

How to apply

Three ways in.

Students should apply through Handshake so your school sees the application. Everyone else can email us directly. Either way, a resume and a short note about your cattle experience is enough to start.

  1. Handshake

    Search for Herd Co. in your school's Handshake portal and apply to the posting. We review applications weekly during the recruiting season.

    Open Handshake

  2. Email

    Send a resume and the position name to becki@herdco.net. We reply to every application within two weeks.

  3. Stop by

    Experienced hands are welcome to call ahead and visit the yard. Ask for Becki at (402) 482-5931.
