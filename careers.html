Accountant / Controller

Full time, in office Bartlett, NE Competitive pay, based on experience 401(k) with employer match Health and dental PTO

You'll handle day-to-day accounting for the feedyard and affiliated entities, including a certified organic grain farming operation, reporting to the CFO. You'd join a small, capable team where your work directly supports real operations: cattle, crops, and the customers who own them. Hybrid flexibility is possible for the right candidate. Bartlett is commutable from Albion, Spalding, O'Neill, Ord, and Burwell.

Apply on Handshake

The role

  • Accounts payable and receivable, including customer cattle billing and feed statements
  • Bank reconciliations and general ledger account reconciliations
  • Month-end close support: clearing accounts, accruals, working papers
  • Payroll processing support and related filings
  • Vendor management, 1099s, and records maintenance
  • Assisting with annual audit and review prep alongside our outside CPA firm

What we're looking for

  • 2+ years of bookkeeping or accounting experience, or an accounting degree or associate degree with strong fundamentals. We will train the right person.
  • Solid Excel skills: comfortable with formulas, lookups, and clean workbook habits
  • Accuracy and follow-through. You notice when something doesn't tie out.
  • Comfortable learning new systems. We use Turnkey FAMS feedyard accounting software; nobody comes in knowing it, and we'll teach you.
  • Ag background helpful but not required

Why this job

  • Stable, year-round work with a growing multi-entity operation
  • Real variety. This isn't data entry; you'll touch cattle accounting, crop accounting, payroll, and banking.
  • Direct mentorship from the CFO with room to grow into a senior accounting role
  • Small-office culture: no bureaucracy, your work is visible and valued
