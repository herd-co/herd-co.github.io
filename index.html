Commercial cattle feedyard · Wheeler County, NE

Feeding cattle in the heart of the Sandhills.

Herd Co. is a family-scale commercial feedyard in Bartlett, Nebraska. We finish cattle for customers across the region and market fed cattle to packers including JBS in Grand Island.

The operation

One yard, four jobs.

A feedyard is a small town of specialists. Cattle arrive as feeders from ranches and sale barns, spend several months on a ration built from local corn and forage, and leave finished for harvest. Every day depends on the pens, the mill, the doctor barn, and the office working together.

Cattle & pens

Pen riders check every pen every day on horseback. Processing crews receive incoming cattle, and the yard crew keeps pens, bunks, and water tanks in shape through Nebraska winters and summers.

Feed mill & delivery

The mill turns corn, distillers grains, hay, and supplements into a balanced ration. Feed trucks deliver to the bunks on a set schedule, and bunk readers adjust calls so cattle are fed to appetite, not to waste.

Animal health

Health crews work with our consulting veterinarian on vaccination, treatment protocols, and records. Low-stress handling and good records protect the cattle and the people working around them.

Office & marketing

The office tracks every lot from arrival to close-out: feed billing, cattle inventory, customer statements, risk management, and marketing fed cattle to packers on grid and cash.

How we work

What we expect of ourselves.

Cattle do not take weekends off, and neither does the weather. We hire people who take that seriously and we back them with steady work, fair pay, and a chance to learn every side of the business.

  • Cattle first

    Bunks fed on time, sick cattle pulled early, pens kept dry. The animals come before the paperwork.

  • Learn the whole yard

    Interns and new hires rotate through pens, mill, health, and office so they understand how each piece affects a close-out.

  • Straight dealing

    Customers get honest close-outs and returned calls. Employees get clear expectations and a boss who shows up.
