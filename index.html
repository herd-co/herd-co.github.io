How we work

What we expect of ourselves.

Cattle do not take weekends off, and neither does the weather. We hire people who take that seriously and we back them with steady work, fair pay, and a chance to learn every side of the business.

  • Cattle first

    Bunks fed on time, sick cattle pulled early, pens kept dry. The animals come before the paperwork.

  • Learn the whole yard

    Interns and new hires rotate through pens, mill, health, and office so they understand how each piece affects a close-out.

  • Straight dealing

    Customers get honest close-outs and returned calls. Employees get clear expectations and a boss who shows up.

Careers

We recruit students and recent graduates in animal science, agronomy, ag business, and accounting through Handshake, and we hire experienced feedyard hands year-round. Internships run through the summer with housing help available.
