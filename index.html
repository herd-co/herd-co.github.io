Commercial cattle feedyard · Wheeler County, NE

Feeding cattle in the heart of the Sandhills.

Herd Co. is a 90,000-head commercial feedyard in Bartlett, Nebraska. We finish cattle for customers across the region and run a professional office that supports the yard.

Where we are

Bartlett, Nebraska.

The yard sits in Wheeler County on the eastern edge of the Sandhills, about 90 minutes north of Grand Island. The office is commutable from Albion, Spalding, O'Neill, Ord, and Burwell.

How we work

What we expect of ourselves.

Cattle do not take weekends off, and neither does the weather. We hire people who take that seriously and we back them with steady work, fair pay, and a chance to learn every side of the business.

  • Cattle first

    Bunks fed on time, sick cattle pulled early, pens kept dry. The animals come before the paperwork.

  • Learn the whole yard

    New hires learn how the pens, the mill, animal health, and the office each affect a close-out, whatever seat they sit in.

  • Straight dealing

    Customers get honest close-outs and returned calls. Employees get clear expectations and a boss who shows up.
